About WeatherNow

Precision Meteorology at Your Fingertips

WeatherNow delivers accurate, hyper-local meteorological forecasts by integrating real-time telemetry from OpenWeatherMap's global network of radar stations, weather satellites, and atmospheric models.

Real-Time Live Telemetry

Instantaneous tracking of temperature, feels-like metrics, barometric air pressure, humidity, and high-resolution wind vectors.

One-Touch Geolocation

Browser-based coordinate detection via the HTML5 Geolocation API, querying instant local weather without persistent tracking.

5-Day Predictive Analysis

Comprehensive synoptic forecast aggregating daily temperature maximums, minimums, and weather conditions across 120 hours.

How to Connect Your OpenWeatherMap API Key

1
Get a Free Key: Sign up for a free account at openweathermap.org and generate an API key in your account dashboard.
2
Option A (In Browser): Click the "API Key" button in the top navigation and paste your key. It saves directly in your browser.
3
Option B (In Code): Open script.js and update const API_KEY = "YOUR_API_KEY"; with your 32-character key.
